# Changelog

## Unreleased
- **Library boxes:** no play time and no "Played" tag on the box (just the player count); the tagline on Fossil Frenzy's cover now fits inside its oval; the shelf no longer pull-to-refreshes or rubber-bands.
- **How to play is now mostly pictures:** four little illustrated cards (Peek, Flip, Match, Bank) with a line each, two one-line reminders (gems, winning), a button to the Fossils panel, and the fine print (judgment calls) folded under "The fine print". Under 110 words on the first screen, and it fits without scrolling on an iPad.
- **Housekeeping (no change to play):** removed dead code (an always-true peek flag, an unused footer, an unused style, the archived two-rule-set report) and a 30-second wasted wait in the sound test.
- **The Tabletop Library shelf is now a shelf of board-game boxes.** Each game is a tall box with its own cover art (Fossil Frenzy: the real board of fossil tiles, a few turned up in the players' colours, with its title) and a small info band (players, time) — no paragraph of text. Boxes sit slightly askew with a thick edge and press down when tapped. Each game supplies its own art (`art` in the catalogue); Dig Dig Boom has a placeholder cover.
- **Way back to the library** from Fossil Frenzy's home screen is now a clear, tappable chip in the very top-left corner.
- **A turn is a peek and a flip.** Once you peek you must flip another fossil: there is no End turn (and no banking) after a peek, and no End turn at the start of a turn. After you flip, **a tap anywhere ends your turn** (the End turn button stays as a cue). Opponents follow the same rule.
- **The dashed ring on a peeked space is neat now:** three equal dashes per side, with the gaps on the corners, so nothing piles up.
- **Gems show what they are worth** (12): under the gem on its fossil, under it on the set you hold (the thing you tap to bank) and on the end screen — the same number that banking scores. **☰ menu button is bigger.** In Settings, **Sound effects** now come before Prompts.
- **Fossils panel:** ☰ menu → **Fossils** shows how many of each number (and gems) are in the game you are playing, read from that game's own data so it follows the level. It deliberately does not show what is left — remembering that is the game. How to play points to it.
- **Layout:** the ☰ menu button moved to the board's bottom-left corner (End turn stays bottom-right), so the two score boxes at the top now span the whole width. The menu opens above the button.
- **Sound effects** (new `sound` script + `GAME_DATA.sound` recipes; nothing to download, works offline): a soft note for a peek, a "tak" for a flip, a chime for a match, a gentle low note for a miss, a sparkle for gems, a coin for a bank, a small "hm" when the opponent slips, and short tunes for winning, losing and unlocking a level. They play for the opponent's moves too, as each is shown. **Sound On/Off** is in Settings (☰ menu → Settings); it is remembered on the device. Sound starts only after your first tap (iPad Safari requires that), stops when the page is hidden, and the iPad's silent switch still mutes it.
- **Settings:** the unlock warning is reworded ("Unlock all opponents?" — what it does, why the campaign is the intended way, that it can be undone). **Reset campaign** is always available (asks first): it forgets every win and any "unlock all". The old "Go back to unlocking by winning" button is gone.
- **Opponent no longer repeats a failed move.** When its memory slips it now taps somewhere it does not already know holds something else, and then drops the belief it just failed on — so Beginner can't try the same wrong match turn after turn. (Checked over 300 games: none repeat three turns running now.)
- **Tapping the fossil you peeked at does nothing** — it no longer hides the number or animates.
- **Win screen:** the winner's card uses that player's own colour for its border (no more green score).
- **Privacy double-check:** a new test watches every change to the board across whole games, animated and not, and fails if an opponent's peeked number ever shows. (It also fails if the rule is deliberately broken, so it does detect leaks.) None found.
- Levels re-tuned again after the slip fix: Memory 5.2 / 7.0 / 8.5 / 9.3 / 9.85; each level beats the one below about 70–80% of the time.
- **Opponents retuned:** the Focus skill (random moves) is gone — opponents are weaker only through fading memory and weaker skills. Normal and up now remember what they have just seen much better, so they make the matches they should; the five levels climb in steps of about 70–78% head to head. The title board's flipping tiles are now never more than two face up at once.
- **Sets you haven't banked are sorted highest to lowest**, both in your score box during play and on the end screen's "Matched, not banked" row.
- **End screen:** one headline, not two. "You beat Novice!" stays the big message; "Normal unlocked" is now a quiet line beneath it (no longer an orange card that looked like a button). Beating Master reads "Campaign complete!" as the headline.
- **End screen:** the final scores are much bigger (the winner's in green) and the names are larger.
- **Opponent's bank banner** now says only who banked and how much ("Beginner banked +7"); the "Score now" line is gone. **Scores in the top bar are bigger** (the numbers and the names).
- **Menus:** the title board no longer has the red dotted ring; the title line is just "Peek. Flip. Match. Bank."; the Campaign button drops its subtitle and shows only the progress dots; the Campaign level list is narrower so each name sits next to its "Beaten"/lock, and an open level shows nothing on the right (no "Ready").
- **No bonus die:** banking now scores the set once — the d6 chain (+1 for every 6) is gone from the rules, the AI and the screens. Saved games from before this change are cleared.
- **Quieter table:** the colour key (concealed / public) above the board is gone. **Menu:** "Back to the start" is now "Main Menu", and "Resume" is removed — tap anywhere outside the menu to close it.
- **Home screen:** back to the original picture — the real board with a few tiles flipping over by themselves on a loop. The scripted demo game (and its small scores) is gone. The home screen is still one screen with no scrolling.
- **The opponents are rebuilt (AI).** One decision engine now plays every opponent, from the perfect player down to the Beginner, by turning abilities up or down: **memory** (fades every turn; a slip taps a random other spot), **valueSense**, **leadSense**, **endSense**, **setsSense**, and banking habits for the easy levels. Each campaign level is just a list of those abilities in the data block, and there is a `random` profile for a future tutorial. The levels are re-set so each beats the one below it, and **Beginner is deliberately easy** (it forgets a lot, is overconfident, goes for gem sets whatever it gives away, and makes a random move one time in five). Every aspect of an opponent is now a **named skill scored 0–10** (Memory, Self-awareness, Value sense, Lead sense, Endgame sense, Set tracking, Gem sense, Focus), and the five levels were set for a **smooth climb** — each beats the one below it by about the same margin. A memory slip is often a near miss, and an opponent that slips mutters "Hm…" on its score box. The perfect AI banks good sets early by default and only goes for a third gem when it knows where it is. Simulations (REPORT.md) and the design notes (`docs/AI.md`) explain what mattered: memory most, then knowing how long the game has left. **Saved games from before this change are cleared** (the opponent's saved format changed).
- **Home screen demo:** each turn now leaves its two tiles face up until the other colour flips its first tile — then a miss flips face down and a match vanishes — so the board never goes blank between turns.
- **Fossil values:** the 10 and 11 are now a **9 and a 10**, and gems are worth **12** (was 16), so a couple of big fossils don't decide the game. Simulations re-run: scores are a little lower and closer together (REPORT.md). Saved games from before this change are cleared (the rules changed).
- **Settings:** a new Settings panel (home screen and in-game ☰ menu) with the prompts choice and **Unlock everything** — it warns that playing through the campaign is the recommended way, then opens every level. Remembered on the device; can be switched back.
- **End screen:** after a campaign win you get **Next level** and **Main menu** (no replay); after a loss, **Try again** and **Main menu**. Each player's fossils are two labelled rows — *Banked* and *Matched, not banked* — and the "+1 / +2" is gone. **Unlocks are celebrated** with a big card and confetti instead of small print.
- **Playtest round 4 (Fossil Frenzy):** the home-screen board now **plays a whole demo game by itself** (blue vs red, rings and vanishing tiles like the real thing, then a pause and a new game), and the home screen is **one screen with no scrolling**. **Held sets are big raised buttons** with a "Tap to bank" label when you can bank. The **opponent's bank banner is a button** — tap it, or anywhere, to go on. Removed "no sets" and "…is thinking". Your own peeked tile no longer has a stray brown dashed edge. The **end-of-game screen** now has two clear buttons — Play again (or Next level) and Main menu — instead of a small "Levels" link.
- **Playtest round 3 (Fossil Frenzy):** a much livelier **home screen** — Campaign first, over a picture of the board with tiles flipping by themselves. **No Continue:** an opponent's turn stays on the board until you tap a fossil, and that tap starts your turn. The opponent's **peek shows instantly** and its flip follows in well under a second. **Sets live in one place** — under each score at the top, where you also tap to bank — and the side panel is gone. A clear **banner when the opponent banks** (what it banked, what it scored, its new score). Smaller words: "no sets", and the campaign screen no longer explains the locks.
- **Icons:** the library and the game now have a proper icon (a fossil tile with an ammonite spiral) for iPad Favorites, the Home Screen and browser tabs. `assets/` holds it; `tools/make-icons.js` redraws it.
- **Playtest round 2 (Fossil Frenzy):** no more "Who goes first?" screen — the order is random and a card names whose turn it is before they tap (every turn in Pass & Play). **Scores are big** across the top, the current player lit up. **Opponents' turns are animated** one move at a time: a peek (the fossil lifts and tilts, the number stays hidden), then the flip; tap to skip ahead. **Two gems:** tapping a third fossil is enough — no need to tap your own gem first. **Banking ends your turn** immediately. A **☰ menu** replaces the ? and ↺ buttons: How to play, Retry (asks first, then restarts at once with the same players/level) and Back to the start.
- **The app is the Tabletop Library** (renamed; Fossil Frenzy remains the name of the game inside it): package, Cloudflare config (`tabletop-library`), links in the docs and the game's browser-storage keys now use the new name. **Saved games and campaign progress made under the old name are discarded.** The live address is now `https://tabletop-library.patrick-blim-hockberger.workers.dev/` (a new Cloudflare project named `tabletop-library`); the GitHub repository keeps its old name on purpose. `docs/PLAN.md` lists every place the name lives.
- **Fossil Frenzy is now one rule set** (owner decision). The original rulebook mode — movement, pieces, corner delivery sites — and the "movement & delivery" toggle are gone. A turn is: first tap a private peek, second tap a public flip; or bank one set.
- **Pass & Play for 2–4 people** (humans only) **or Campaign**: five opponents — Beginner, Novice, Normal, Challenging, Master — shown by name only (no percentages). Only Beginner is open at first; the others are greyed out with a lock. Win outright to unlock the next; progress is remembered on the device. `?unlock` on the address opens every level for testing.
- The game's address and saved games: a game saved by an earlier version is cleared with a quiet note (the rules changed); campaign progress starts fresh.
- **Library:** the designer is credited once, at the top — "Games designed by Patrick Blim-Hockberger" — not under each game.
- REPORT.md rewritten for the single rule set and now includes how hard each campaign level is; the old two-rule-set comparison is archived in `games/fossil-frenzy/docs/archive/`.
- **Quieter prompts:** the line of instructions above the board shows until a device has finished one game, then goes quiet; Auto / On / Off in the help panel.
- **End screen:** each player's fossils laid out face up with their score and the winner marked, instead of a stats table. Undelivered/unbanked sets show dimmed and struck through.
- **Save and resume (Fossil Frenzy):** a game in progress is saved after every action and resumes after a reload — mid-turn, mid-peek, or with a bot's turn on the board. A save made under different rules (after an update that changes the rules) is cleared with a quiet note instead of resuming wrongly. A finished game or "new game" clears the save.
- **Tabletop Library:** a front page with a shelf of game boxes; Fossil Frenzy moved to `games/fossil-frenzy/` and links back to the library. **Fossil Frenzy's address changes** to `…/games/fossil-frenzy/`; the site's root is now the library. Dig Dig Boom has a placeholder entry, off the shelf until it has an address.
- **Redesign mode (owner feedback):** a bank takes ONE set of your choice and scores the number on its fossil (gem 16), then the d6 bonus chain. Previously every held set scored 1 point at once.
- **Bot turns show their work:** a dashed ring marks the space a bot peeked at (number hidden), a solid ring the space it flipped, and a match stays face up until the next tap.
- Bots bank as soon as they hold a set; REPORT.md re-run (the two rule sets now play very similarly on every measure).
- Build stamp: each deployed copy shows its commit, date and branch (setup screen and How to play).
- Deploys are gated by the tests: `npm run build` runs `npm test` first.
- `docs/PLAN.md`: the plan for the collection, code practices and tabletop design rules.

## Fossil Frenzy v0.1.0 — 2026-10-18 (commit `4d78deb`)
First playable release.
- Hot-seat play for 2–6 players on one screen, touch-first for iPad.
- Two rule sets from one toggle: the original rulebook (movement and delivery) and Patrick's redesign (peek, flip, bank).
- Bots with adjustable memory (Beginner–Master presets).
- 104 automated tests, 1,000-game bot simulations (`REPORT.md`), every judgment call in `DECISIONS.md`.
- Hosted on Cloudflare: `main` is live, every branch gets a preview link.
