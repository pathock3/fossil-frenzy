# One More… Rulebook
(From Patrick's Google Drive — "One More... Rulebook", last updated Sep. 2024. This is the source for the build.)

Players: 2-6 · Duration: 10 min. · Ages: 10+ · Designer: Patrick Blim-Hockberger · Special Thanks: Caleb Awe, Emily Blim-Hockberger, Phil Hockberger, Hanna Warmka, Robert Warmka · Creation Date: Sep. 2021

## Game Overview
Collect as many gems as you can, but try not to go boom! In this quick push your luck game, you must decide when to save your gems and when to risk digging for just one more...

## Components
38 gem cards
6 boom cards

## Setup
Shuffle all cards together and place them face down to form the draw pile. Randomly determine a turn order.

## Gameplay
On your turn, draw cards until you either decide to save or go boom!

If you draw a boom card, you immediately go boom, losing all gems you collected this turn. Place all cards drawn this turn in a pile, boom card facing up, off to the side, so that all players can see how many booms have been drawn so far. Now it is the next player's turn.

If you draw a gem card, you have a choice. Will you save your gems, placing them in a pile near you where they can never be lost, and ending your turn? Or will you draw again, hoping to draw more gems, but risking losing what you have drawn so far this turn? As long as you keep drawing gems, you may draw as many cards as you like.

Continue playing in turn order. Whenever the 6th and final boom card is drawn, refresh the draw pile by shuffling all cards not saved by players to form a new draw pile and continue playing. If a player realizes that all cards left in the draw pile are booms, they may refresh the draw pile and continue their turn.

## Winning the Game
In a 3-6 player game, the game ends when all gems have been collected and the only cards remaining are the 6 boom cards. Whichever player has the most gems wins! If playing again, the player who scored the fewest points goes first, second fewest goes second, and so on.

In a 2-player game, the first player to collect 20 gems wins! If both players reach 19, they tie. If playing again, the loser goes first.

## Drinking Game Variant
When you go boom, drink!

## Expansion: Booby Prizes
Components: 16 booby prize cards.
Setup: Shuffle the booby prize deck and place them face down. Set up the rest of the game as normal.
Gameplay: Every time you go boom, draw 2 cards from the booby prize deck and choose 1 to keep. Play the rest of the game as normal.
(The rulebook does not list what the 16 booby prize cards are.)
