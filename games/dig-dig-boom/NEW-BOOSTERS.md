# New Booster cards: the design spaces v2.7 opened up

`NEW-BOOSTERS.csv` holds 32 proposed cards (ids PW-60…, ON-60…, IM-60…). They are **not on the sheet yet**: say the word and I add them. The game already plays all of them (`tests/newcards.test.js`).

## The design spaces
1. **Changing the die after it is rolled.** "+N to your result" lets one Power card pull the next one in (a chain: 4 → 6 → 9 reaches the Pet). Cards: Ladder Lizard, Spring Hare, Jetpack Gull, Tailwind Swift, Mirror Mantis, Daredevil Gerbil (pay 1 Health for +3).
2. **Rules of Extra Rolls** (cap 4, "replace your result" is now a rule cards can bend). Loaded Dice (keep the higher die), Free Spin (the first one costs nothing), Hoarder's Chest (+1 per Extra Roll held), Overflow Valve (pays when an Extra Roll does not fit), Reroll Rebate (pays when you spend one).
3. **The number on the die itself.** Snake Eyes (roll a 1), Lucky Seven, Boxcars (roll a 10) react to the die, Extra Rolls included. Consolation Prize / Hot Streak react to the result you face a Monster with (low / 10+).
4. **Shaping the damage.** Bump Stop (at most 5), Tough Hide (2 or less is nothing), Lucky Number (every 3rd Monster is harmless), Smoke Bomb (next Monster halved), Decoy (next Monster harmless).
5. **Defeating, not just surviving.** Brave Terrier (≤3 damage) and Bulldog Bouncer (≤6) defeat small Monsters: with Bounty Poster, Monster Jerky and Victory Bell they pay.
6. **One-shot ("break") cards.** Phoenix Feather: the first time a Monster would BOOM you, your Health becomes 4 and the card breaks (like Panic Button).
7. **Moving Monsters.** Trapdoor (the next Monster in the Mine goes to the bottom), Dull Claws (every Monster set steps back one on its damage ladder).
8. **Health thresholds.** Full Tank (+2 to rolls at 8+ Health) mirrors Redline (low Health). Panic Possum (≤3 Health: 2 Extra Rolls), Crimson Vial (3 Health for 3 Extra Rolls).
9. **Existing, now cheaper to fill:** Crowd Pleaser (3+ cards trigger), Rejuvenating Spring, Sampler.

## Not built yet (needs a new tap on the table, and a decision for the AI)
- "Once per Monster, you may lose 2 Health to get +3 to your result" (pay Health to bend the die after the roll).
- "Swap your result for 11 minus it" (flip the die), "roll two dice and keep the higher on your first roll".
- A tap-to-use card that changes which Monster you face (swap the Monster with the next card).

## Rebalancing, the second time (measured, damped, repeated)
**How it is measured** (`tools/cardvalue.js`, driven by `tools/rebalance.js`): a card's value is how many more Monsters you survive before the BOOM with the card in your Starter Kit than with a gem in its place, on the same Mines and dice, averaged over 120 random kits (the card, one other Booster card, and three more cards you own, shuffled into the Mine), with the AI's quick policy playing every fight. One pass over all 97 cards takes about two minutes; the noise on a card's value is about ±0.07 Monsters (the spread between cards is about ±0.8).
**How it keeps from just shuffling the order:** a band is set from the first measurement (floor 0.25, ceiling 1.8 Monsters). Only cards below the floor are buffed, only up to halfway to the floor and never past the middle of the pack; only cards above the ceiling are nerfed, halfway down and never below the middle. Cards inside the band are never touched, so a buffed card cannot overtake the others and a nerfed one cannot fall below them. Each step is one number (a number in the text, or a Power card's trigger number); the step whose measured value lands nearest the aim is chosen. Then measure again, repeat. Cards that work through other cards, through what you keep, or by dodging Monsters (Echo, Rewind Tape, Wishing Well, Wormhole, Crystal Ball, Salvage Yard, Cloning Machine, the Mystery Boxes, Surprise Package, Magpie, Old Map, Cave Scout, Stink Bomb, Trapdoor, Fuzzy Radar, Sampler, All In) are measured but not changed by the tool.
**Result:** 7 rounds of steps took the strongest cards from +3.99 (Picnic Ant) / +3.57 (Jetpack Gull) / +3.20 (Mirror Mantis) down to about +1.4…+1.8, and brought the weakest up to about +0.25. A second measurement on different kits and dice agreed (difference 0.06) and had the same shape. As a cross-check against real play, 480 simulated matches (AI vs AI, 4 rounds, the 32 new cards in the Booster Deck): the new cards' median pick rate is 41% against 41% for the sheet's cards, and every card is picked between 26% and 69% of the time it is offered (before: 20–70%; All In stays near 10% on purpose, it is a gamble).
**Changes to the sheet's cards** (all written to your sheet): Nurse Newt trigger 1→2; Picnic Ant 3→9; Moss Sloth 6→7; Snack Raccoon +4→+3 Health; Nervous Mouse 5 Health or less; Falcon Finisher defeats on 6 or more; Applause Meter +2 Rerolls; Crumple Zone +2 Rerolls; Disco Ball +2 Health; Floor Jack below 6 becomes 6; Nurse Bot gain 2 more; Duct Tape +2 per Ongoing card; Sneak Attack "Dig 2 cards. Defeat any Monster you dig". (Earlier this session: All In, Thorned Gadget, Giant Slayer, Reset Button, Risky Shortcut, Cowcatcher.)
**Changes to the proposed new cards:** Spring Hare 7, +1; Jetpack Gull 7, +1; Tailwind Swift 8; Mirror Mantis 9; Lucky Number every 4th; Phoenix Feather sets Health to 1 (the AI took it 67% of the time at 2); Trapdoor now costs 1 Health (69%: the survival measure cannot see Monster-dodging, the AI can); Bump Stop at most 4; Panic Possum 5 Health or less; Crowd Pleaser +3; Falcon, Crimson Vial +4 Rerolls, Hot Streak 7 or more; Overflow Valve became **Pressure Valve** ("Whenever you gain an Extra Roll, +1 Health") and Loaded Dice also makes the first Extra Roll free (both were too rare to matter).
**What the measure cannot see:** gems (a Dig that goes deeper but survives the same Monsters), the Leaderboard and banking decisions, what a card is worth to somebody who is hoarding the pack's other cards. It is a guide to balance, not a verdict: the AI's pick rates (tools/picks.js) are the cross-check.

To go again after any change: `node games/dig-dig-boom/tools/rebalance.js init <dir>`, then `loop <dir> 3`, then `publish <dir>` (copies the working copies back over the fixture and NEW-BOOSTERS.csv; the sheet is then updated by hand).
