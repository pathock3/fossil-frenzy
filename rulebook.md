# Fossil Frenzy rules

Alt titles: Fossils, Fossil Hunt, Hunters, Hunting, Playing with Matches

Designer: Patrick Hockberger
Created: Jan. 2019
Version: .2, 3/24/19

Players: 2-6
Ages: 6+
Duration: 15-20 min

## Overview

Fossil Frenzy is a fast, family, memory game. You, like so many others, have been drawn to "Dinosaur Valley", the richest deposit of dinosaur fossils ever discovered! Match fossils, make deliveries, and outwit your rivals to score the most points before they are all gone. **For a summary of the rules, read only the bold text!**

## Components

Rulebook, reference card
Game board
6 pieces, dice
31 fossil tokens - six 2s, six 3s, four 5s, four 6s, four 7s, two 10s, two 11s, and three gems worth 16

## Setup

Put the fossil tokens face down on the board and mix them up. **Align one fossil with each space on the board, except for the board's corners.** Each player takes a piece and matching die. **Roll your die and place your piece in the corresponding corner.** If two players roll the same number, whomever rolled it second rolls again. The player who rolled the highest number goes first, and play continues clockwise.

## Play

### MOVEMENT

**Begin your turn by moving your piece 0-3 spaces.** If another player is in your way, you may either go around or push them one space in any direction and move to where they were. However, you may not continue moving after pushing, and you may not bump one player into another.

### PEEKING, FLIPPING, & MATCHING

Note: These rules use the word "fossil" to refer to fossil tokens, some of which depict gems. Your goal is to match the number on the fossil you are on to another on the board. **After moving if you are on a fossil, you may peek at it** so that only you can see what is on it. **If you peek, you must also flip one other fossil on the board for all players to see.** You now have the option to flip the fossil you are standing on. **If they match, place the fossils face up in front of you.** If you flip one of the gems and want to try to match all three, you may flip a second fossil, then the one you are standing on. If you are not on a fossil, you cannot peek, flip, or match.

### DELIVERING FOSSILS

**Fossils are worth nothing until they have been delivered to your assigned research site**, found in the six corners of the board. The number on your die tells you where to make your next delivery. **Once at the site, you may flip one fossil face down and toss its match(es) back in the game box** - your employer takes a 50% cut on all matches. **You may only deliver one fossil at a time**, but you may choose which one. **Now roll your die to get your next delivery assignment.** If you roll the same number where you already are, you may make another deposit and roll again. After delivering a fossil, you cannot move until your next turn. You may peek at your face down fossils at any time.

So turns involve some combination of moving, peeking, flipping, matching, and delivering. Now it is the next player's turn!

## End & Scoring

**The game immediately ends when all fossil tokens have been matched.** Add up all your points from face down fossils. A gem is worth 16. **Any face up fossils are worth 0 points.** **Whomever has the highest score wins!** If there is a tie, whomever has the most fossils wins. If still tied, whomever has the highest scoring fossil wins.
